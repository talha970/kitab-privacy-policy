Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

Kitab is a local-first reader. You can read without an account. Optional Kitab Pro sync connects to your Google account and stores the data you choose to sync in your private Google Drive application-data folder. Kitab contains no ads or analytics and does not send your books or reading activity to the developer.

Information Kitab handles

Kitab handles only the content and settings needed to provide its reading features:

  • Books, PDFs, comics, and images that you choose to open or import
  • Publication metadata and generated cover images
  • Reading position, progress, bookmarks, highlights, and excerpts connected to highlights
  • Reading preferences such as theme, typography, layout, and brightness

This information is stored in Kitab's private app storage on your device. If you enable Google Drive sync, Kitab also stores your Google account email address and account identifier locally to identify the connected account. Kitab does not request your precise location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or payment-card details.

How information is used

Your imported content and reading data are used only to open publications, display your library, restore your position, and provide the reading features you choose to use. Kitab does not sell personal information or use it for advertising. If you enable sync, the selected data is transmitted directly to Google Drive to provide that feature; it is not sent to a developer-operated server.

Network access and third parties

Local reading works offline. Kitab uses internet access for optional Google Drive sync and Google Play services. It has no developer-operated storage server, advertising service, or analytics service. Google processes account, Drive, and purchase information under its own policies.

Android and the store from which you installed Kitab may independently process information needed to distribute the app, provide operating-system features, or report platform diagnostics. That activity is controlled by those providers and their privacy policies, not by Kitab.

Optional Google Drive sync

When you connect Google Drive, Kitab requests openid and email to identify your account, and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata to create, read, update, and delete Kitab data in the private application-data folder. Kitab cannot browse unrelated Drive files.

You choose which categories to sync: books, reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, library metadata, and reader settings. Sync records also include timestamps and an app-generated device identifier to coordinate changes between devices. The data is used only for sync, backup, and restoration.

Google authorization access tokens are used for requests and are not persisted by Kitab. Your Google password is handled by Google. Google user data is not used for advertising, sold, or used to train AI models.

Kitab's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting and deleting cloud data

Disconnect Google Drive in Kitab's Sync & Backup settings to stop sync and request revocation of access. You can also revoke access through your Google account's third-party connections. Disconnecting or uninstalling Kitab does not itself delete the existing cloud backup. To delete stored cloud data, use Google Drive's Settings > Manage apps and delete Kitab's hidden app data. Local copies remain subject to the device controls described below.

Kitab Pro purchases

Google Play handles Kitab Pro purchases. Kitab checks purchase status and acknowledges purchases through Google Play Billing to unlock Pro features. Kitab does not receive payment-card details. Google Play controls payment processing and purchase records.

Document access

Kitab uses Android's system document picker or an “Open with” action to access only files you select. A selected publication is copied into the app's private storage so it remains available on your shelf. Kitab does not request broad access to all files on your device.

Backups and retention

Depending on your device and Android backup settings, the operating system may include Kitab's local app data in a device or cloud backup associated with your device account. Kitab's developer does not operate or receive those backups.

Your data remains on your device until you remove a publication, clear Kitab's storage, or uninstall the app, subject to any backups managed by your operating system. Removing a publication also removes its locally stored reading position and annotations from Kitab.

Children's privacy

Kitab does not use advertising or analytics and does not create advertising profiles. Optional Google Drive sync processes the connected account information and selected reading data described above.

Security

Imported publications and reading data are kept in Android's private app storage. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed absolutely secure, so you should also protect access to your device and device backups.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Kitab's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when an update is published.
