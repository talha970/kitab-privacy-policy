Read your way

Choose paginated or continuous EPUB reading, adjust fonts and spacing, and switch between light, sepia, and dark themes. Find passages with in-book search and return to them with bookmarks and highlights.

A library that stays with you

Import your own EPUBs, PDFs, CBZ comics, and images. Kitab saves your reading position so you can pick up where you left off. Local reading works without a Google account or an internet connection.

Optional private sync with Kitab Pro

Connect your Google account to sync the categories you choose across devices: books, reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, library metadata, and reader settings. Kitab stores this data in your private Google Drive application-data folder and cannot browse unrelated Drive files.

Google authentication is used only for this optional connection. Your Google password stays with Google. Read our privacy policy for details about data access, storage, and disconnecting.
